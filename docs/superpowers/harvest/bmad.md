# BMAD-METHOD harvest — elicitation / coaching / brainstorming

Read-only research pass comparing current BMAD-METHOD (`/workspace/dev/ai-tools/BMAD-METHOD`,
branch `main`) against `dev-workflows`'s authoring pipeline (`/idea`, `/create-vi`, `/create-ard`,
`/specify`, and the shared `references/grilling-technique.md`), to decide whether ~242
commits / 120 days of BMAD churn contain anything worth folding in.

**Bottom line:** most of the churn is BMAD's own architectural refactor (skill consolidation,
`_bmad/scripts/*.py`, `customize.toml`, memlog standardization) — not content upgrades that
transfer. The actual *content* improvements are narrow and mostly already convergently present
in our artifacts (e.g. our `vi-reviewer`'s "substance over theater" dimension is near-verbatim
BMAD's PRD-validation-checklist dimension of the same name; our VI's Spine + Adapt-in Menu is
already named after BMAD's own PRD-template terminology). Net: 4 small Adapt items, everything
else Skip — either architecturally incompatible (memlog/persona/Canvas-UI-bound) or a different
job (external market research, generic multi-content review) than our Jira/VI/ARD/spec pipeline
does.

## Summary of the 4 concrete Adapt items

1. **Terminology-precision forcing move** (from `bmad-forge-idea`) → add one bullet to `references/grilling-technique.md` Mechanics: name an overloaded/fuzzy term (e.g. "user" vs. "buyer" vs. "payer") and force a precise choice before continuing. Touches all grill callers (`/idea`, `/create-vi`, `/create-ard`, `/specify`, `/design`). Effort S.
2. **Named gap-categories from the elicitation catalog** (Pre-mortem Analysis, Assumption Audit, Second-Order Thinking, Steelmanning) → fold into the ambiguity-taxonomy / self-consistency-check language already present in `/create-vi` Phase 3, `/create-ard` Phase 4, `/specify` Phase 5 — as additional gap types to rank by Impact×Uncertainty, not a user-facing menu. Effort S.
3. **Counter-metrics** (`SM-C1`-style) → add as an optional sub-bullet under `references/vi-format.md`'s `## Success Metrics` spine section, and a corresponding `vi-reviewer.md` dimension note ("a Primary SM with no counter-metric where gaming is plausible → NIT/MINOR, non-blocking"). Effort S.
4. **Implicit-branch-of-an-enum heuristic** (from `lens-edge-case-hunter`) → add one bullet to `references/specification-format.md`'s Stage 2 coverage-scan list, generalizing the existing paired-state-transition check from binary to N-ary enum/status fields. Effort S.

(Adjacent, out of the requested idea/create-vi/create-ard/specify scope but noted since `bmad-review`
was an explicit source: the "missing-adoption gap" concept could sharpen `agents/code-review.md`'s
edge-case dimension for `/implement`/`/vuln`/`/upgrade`. Effort S, follow-up candidate.)

All four in-scope items are additive, single-file, backward-compatible edits (no new agents, no new
phases, no new infrastructure) — each could land as its own small PR if the user wants to pursue them.
