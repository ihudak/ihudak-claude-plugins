# References reference

`pm-workflows` bundles nine files under `references/`, all top-level markdown, no bundled subtrees. This page enumerates every one, grouped by concern below.

The shared corpus every plugin in this family reads — the addressing grammar, the git and phase-handoff entry points, model routing, escalation and triage, cost/feedback/follow-up emission, the grounding and grilling conventions, and the PRD format — ships in the companion `workflows-core` plugin and is enumerated on its own references page, reached through the loader skill rather than by path. What is listed here is what `pm-workflows` itself carries.

## BRD-to-PRD route formats

The canonical structure each BRD-route artifact is authored and reviewed against.

- `brd-format.md` — what a BRD is (customer-supplied, immutable once intaken, markdown only), the `kind:`/`key:` header every inventory opens with — carried there and never in the immutable source — the `[BR#n]` inventory row shape and the split-on-multiple-obligations rule, the six `[DEF#n]` defect classes, and defect-resolution states; `/brd-intake` authors against it and `brd-reader` extracts against it, `/brd-ground` and `/brd-split` read its defect classes and resolution states, and `/brd-reconcile` writes the `customer-amended` and `withdrawn` resolutions a returned review settles — into the parent's log when it stands on a slice.
- `coverage-ledger-format.md` — the coverage ledger's row shape, its six dispositions (only `unallocated` blocks), the allocation gate `/brd-split` enforces, the D23 roll-up behind its ledger line, the PRD-eligibility rule read from it — satisfiable only on a slice, since a parent BRD is a container whose walk is never offered `covered-here` — the positive test that separates a legacy unprefixed root BRD from a legacy idea-route PRD folder, shared by the four commands that refuse a container, the three refusals an offer naming `/create-prd` has to clear before it names it, and what `covered-by` means downstream — a row another BRD owns leaves this BRD's scope as a subject, so neither its interview nor its package puts it to a human twice; `/brd-intake` writes the ledger against it, `/brd-ground` and `/brd-split` read and update it, and `/brd-reconcile` moves a row on a frozen customer decision without ever allocating one.
- `decision-register-format.md` — the decision register's record shape for a `[VD#n]` (delivery-team) and `[CD#n]` (customer) decision, its five statuses (including the first-class `withdrawn`), the mandatory `argumentation` field, the two causes that may reopen a decision, what `conditional_on` records and makes findable, the D19 rule against resting solely on a `will-change` finding, and the `[AS#n]` assumption every open one of which reaches the customer; `/brd-interview` writes `[VD#n]` and `[AS#n]` records against it and enforces its will-change rule, `brd-package-reviewer` reads them, `/brd-package` surfaces every open assumption and every `conditional_on` position to the customer, and `/brd-reconcile` writes the `[CD#n]` records — the only command that does — supersedes the assumptions they settle, and sweeps every `conditional_on` position in every dependent BRD.
- `interview-tagging.md` — the interview's three question tags (`[G]` answered from grounding by nobody, `[V]` decided by the delivery team with argumentation, `[C]` put to the customer via the review package), the rule each tag fixes about who may answer, the re-tagging of a `[G]` grounding cannot settle, the split of a question carrying two tags, and the rule that closes a round; `/brd-interview` tags every question against it, `brd-package-reviewer` applies its test to one already routed, `/brd-package` routes the `[C]` questions into the customer prompt and gates on its round vocabulary, and `/brd-reconcile` writes the terminal disposition *answered by the customer* that finally closes a round holding one.
- `bundle-packaging.md` — how the customer-facing bundle is built plugin-free (D12): the four rules that keep it followable by a reviewer with nothing installed, including locating documents by filename search rather than by path; the de-Obsidianising pass that rewrites wikilinks to plain filename references and keeps callouts; the three degradation tiers and what each obliges a returned review to state about its own evidence; the **allow-list of what the bundle may contain** — derived from the rule that a document reaches it only where a prompt part sends the reviewer to it, with the self-review and the delivery note excluded by name — and the one document copied byte for byte rather than rendered, the customer's own source, whose `source_anchor` traceability a rendering pass would break; the delivery note's 200-word ceiling and the two facts it must carry; and the `bundle-<YYYYMMDD>/` directory (D18) that, once handed off, is the permanent record of what was sent. `/brd-package` builds every bundle against it, and `/brd-reconcile` reads a review returned against one — and cites the same section for why nothing inside that committed directory is ever bannered or rewritten afterwards.
- `customer-review-schema.md` — the twelve sections the customer's reviewer fills and sends back, the rule that puts the review's evidence limitations ahead of every claim resting on them, and the D13 rule that the review is exactly one new file with nothing in the package modified; unlike every other reference here its body is rendered verbatim into the customer's prompt, so it names no path, command, agent or skill a reader with nothing installed could not resolve. `/brd-package` inlines its body — from section 2 onward, never the whole file — into the prompt the customer pastes, and `/brd-reconcile` parses a returned review against that same body.

## PRD-ladder formats

The canonical structure each artifact of the idea → PRD → ARD → specification ladder is authored and reviewed against.

- `idea-format.md` — canonical structure and per-section rules for a refined idea brief; `/idea` is the only author and cites it directly, while `/create-prd` consumes the resulting artifact without citing this format doc. It also owns the *Vendored sources* rules `/idea` Phase 4.5 executes: text and markdown sources copied into the folder's reserved `attachments/`, read images into `design/idea-sources/`, the `_NN` collision suffix that is never compounded, and the rule that a link in the brief is repointed only where the target was actually copied. The index that frame set must carry is **not** this file's — the grounding-format reference in `workflows-core` requires it in its §6.1 and formats it in its §6.2, and this file states only what `/idea` contributes to a row.
- `ard-format.md` — canonical structure and rules for an Architecture Requirements/Decision Document — **ard.md**, or **ard-\<area\>.md** for an area-scoped one — plus its `kind:`/`key:` frontmatter; `ard-reviewer` reviews against it and `dev-workflows:ready` reads its `grounded_repos:` frontmatter.
- `specification-format.md` — canonical structure and per-stage rules for a product specification — **specification.md** — plus its `kind:`/`key:` frontmatter; `/specify` authors against it and `spec-reviewer` reviews against it. An embedded snapshot, not a net-new format.

Every one of the nine files above is cited by at least one command or agent. The engineering-design counterpart one altitude down — the format an implementation design is authored against — ships in the companion `dev-workflows` plugin and is not part of this one.
